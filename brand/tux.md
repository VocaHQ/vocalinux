# Tux

Linux mascot used on the Open Graph card.

`tux.svg` is a flat derivative of Larry Ewing's Tux: the original
silhouette, filled with Vocalinux tokens (ink, paper-bright, sun).
No gradients, blurs, or glares. `tux-classic.svg` is the unaltered
Wikimedia vector.

Original drawing by Larry Ewing with The GIMP.
Vector by Larry Ewing, Simon Budig, and Garrett LeSage.
