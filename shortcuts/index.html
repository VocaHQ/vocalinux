Quick Reference

Keyboard Shortcuts & Voice Commands

Master Vocalinux with keyboard shortcuts and voice commands. New installs default to push-to-talk (hold Right Alt), or switch to toggle mode (double-tap), then apply voice commands for editing and formatting.

Quick Start

Hold Right Alt
→Push-to-Talk: Speak While Held

Right Alt is the Option key on Mac-layout keyboards. Toggle mode is also available: double-tap your configured key to start/stop. You can switch modes and keys in Settings.

Voice commands can be enabled or disabled from Settings depending on your workflow.
